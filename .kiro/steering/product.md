# Dark Pattern Manor

A browser game. "Every trick is a ghost. Name it to set the page free."

A haunted website where every ghost is a real dark pattern: the player spots the trick, names it, and the page turns honest beside its haunted twin. Audience: everyone online; UX students second.

Why: people sense manipulation but can't name it (63.8% noticed, 38.2% named it; Wang 2025, Drexel); dark patterns are common (1,818 instances on ~11,000 shopping sites; Mathur et al. 2019); regulators act on them (FTC "Bringing Dark Patterns to Light", 2022; EU DSA Art. 25). 10-15 min, 12 rooms, ~1 min each.

## Core loop per room

1. Haunted page: a fictional brand's site with an everyday task (shown in a task bar). One possessed element behaves like the real dark pattern and gives itself away with small signs: flicker, a cold breath, pushing the cursor.
2. Name it: select the possessed element (pointer, keyboard or screen reader) and pick one of three spell cards. One right, one close relative, one clearly different. A wrong card costs nothing: the light flickers and a whispered hint names the difference.
3. Honest: the ghost flees; the page splits into the haunted version and its honest twin with a Before/After slider; the task now takes one click. A Ghost Guide card opens: two-sentence definition, where you meet it, a US line and an EU line with sources, one protection tip.

After 12 rooms: a 12-line "Ghost hunt in your own browser" checklist and a share card.

## Rooms

1. The Foyer — The Gravemoor Gazette (news) — Visual interference — reject the cookies
2. The Library — Inkbone Books — Trick wording — sign up without the newsletter
3. The Dining Room — Grimsupper (meal kits) — Confirmshaming — close the discount pop-up
4. The Parlour — Gloamline (streaming) — Hidden subscription — start the free trial knowing what comes after
5. The Conservatory — Hollowpost Tickets — Fake urgency — buy the ticket without rushing
6. The Ballroom — Shroudery (clothing) — Fake scarcity — pick the right size
7. The Portrait Gallery — Lanternstay (hotels) — Fake social proof — choose a room by real reviews
8. The Pantry — Mothmilk Market (groceries) — Sneaking — pay only for what you picked
9. The Study — Mildew Drive (cloud storage) — Hidden costs — find the real price
10. The Nursery — Wanderwick (mobile game) — Nagging — keep playing
11. The Attic — Rookery Air (airline) — Preselection — buy only the ticket
12. The Cellar — Bramblegate Fitness (gym) — Hard to cancel — cancel the membership
